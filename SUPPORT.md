# Getting help

- **Using Chistara** (account, credit, documents, the apps): https://hoghogh.ai/contact (signed in: https://hoghogh.ai/app/support), or email info@hoghogh.ai.
- **Connecting Chistara to Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client:** see the guide in
  [chistara-mcp](https://github.com/Chistara/chistara-mcp#connect), and open an issue there if a connection fails.
- **Security issues:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); please don't open a public issue.
